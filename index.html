Queen of Darkness (DE: Königin der Dunkelheit)

Passive Passive Skill Non-Attribute Fusion

Defeating an enemy, grants the Custom Resource Umbra.

Equipping a shotgun activates Queen of Darkness. While active, firearm attacks deal additional damage per shot that hits and grant Domination. When Veil of Night is active, increases the amount of Additional Damage. Hitting an enemy inflicted with Trace or Bleed with a firearm, reloads a certain number of Rounds for the currently equipped weapon.

When Domination is at max stacks, using an active skill instantly Reloads the firearm and removes the effect.

When using Roll, Raven moves farther and retrieves Blade Feathers.

Cooldown / cost / numeric values: not published in the official data.

Shadow Shot (DE: Schattenschuss)

Skill 1 Active Skill Non-Attribute Fusion

Can be used after firing a shotgun a certain number of times to charge. The closer to the target, the greater the damage dealt.

Inflicts Trace on hit enemies. Each Shotgun attack on an enemy with Trace deals additional damage to all enemies inflicted with Trace within range.

Acquires Umbra proportional to the number of enemies hit by the Skill.

Cooldown / cost / numeric values: not published in the official data.

Raven Feather (DE: Rabenfeder)

Skill 2 Active Skill Non-Attribute Fusion

Sends a raven made of shadow flying forward. If the raven hits an enemy or terrain feature, it creates a Blade Feather at the location. If the raven does not create a Blade Feather, refunds a portion of the cooldown.

Blade Feather inflicts Bleed on enemies within range. When reusing the skill, retrieves the Blade Feather and moves to that location. Grants Ambush.

The raven and Blade Feather vanish when too far from Raven.

Cooldown / cost / numeric values: not published in the official data.

Shadow Step (DE: Schattenschritt)

Skill 3 Active Skill Non-Attribute Fusion

Quickly dashes forward. When the dash ends, inflicts Trace on enemies within range and grants Veil of Night.

Veil of Night is removed after negating damage a certain number of times.

Each Shotgun attack on an enemy with Trace deals additional damage to all enemies inflicted with Trace within range.

Cooldown / cost / numeric values: not published in the official data.

Vendetta (DE: Vendetta)

Skill 4 Active Skill Non-Attribute Fusion

Fires a shotgun repeatedly at nearby enemies. Additional input consumes Umbra to perform up to 5 attacks. The final attack deals heavy damage.

The closer to the target, the greater the damage dealt.

Reduces Incoming Damage while attacking, but being hit consumes additional Umbra Gauge. While using the skill, becomes immune to Knockdown and Knockback.

Cooldown / cost / numeric values: not published in the official data.

Story

"Confirmed death of primary surveillance target 'Corvus.' 'Nightfall' has been completely taken over by an unidentified force overnight. Note: Evidence of intervention by an unregistered Arche ability user, aka the 'Ghost,' who has long been a surveillance target. Extreme caution is required."

– An excerpt from a classified report by the Colon Special Forces, Albion Headquarters

Raven grew up as Eleanore, an orphan from Albion's back alleys. When she awakened an Arche ability that let her perfectly assimilate into the shadows, her godfather Corvus – the man who had given her the name Eleanore – made her the sharpest weapon of Nightfall, the most powerful faction in the back alleys.

Corvus' greed for power grew until he sold forbidden artifacts and even helpless orphans to the Vulgus as experimental subjects – a line Eleanore would never cross. She executed Corvus herself, then crushed and unified the fragmented cartels into a new, massive alliance called the Guild. Eleanore the "hunting hound" vanished, and Raven, the Queen of Darkness, came to rule.

Albion's back alleys were divided over her: a filthy traitor who repaid kindness with betrayal, or the Bloody Queen who brought strict order to a lawless land. Still, she asked herself how a life spent hiding in the shadows would differ from staying trapped in the refuge Corvus had created – so she stepped out of the shadows and, without hesitation, stood before the Albion Command that had pursued her for years.

Summary of the official library story (Story tab).

Exclusive Equipment / Ability: Exclusive Arche, Photonic Energy Control

Raven's Arche appears to rule the darkness, but the Magisters determined that her true ability is closer to manipulating photons – strictly limited to photon annihilation.

By annihilating photons she creates spaces devoid of light, and she is believed to convert the energy released during the annihilation into actual physical force. Several aspects defy the Magisters' current understanding of physics; they hoped to research it further, but Raven has refused.

– Final conversation between Corvus and Raven
